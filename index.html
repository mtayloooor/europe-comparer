About Autofill

Autofill computes a rough cost & time estimate for each leg from the distance between its endpoints and the travel method. It only fills empty fields — your own numbers are never overwritten.

  • Cost = a per-km rate × distance (driving ≈ petrol use; train/bus/ferry ≈ typical fares; flights add a base fare).
  • Time = distance ÷ a typical average speed for the method (flights add ~2 h for airport time).
  • Day trips are estimated by car for the one-way hop to the spot (then doubled for the round trip).
